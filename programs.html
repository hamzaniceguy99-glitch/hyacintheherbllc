Program 03 · Infusions, nothing more

Loose-leaf herbal teas

Herbal infusions are drinks. We will describe how one tastes, not what it will do for you, because therapeutic claims about botanicals are both regulated and usually unfounded. If you are pregnant or taking medication, check with a pharmacist first — some botanicals genuinely interact.
