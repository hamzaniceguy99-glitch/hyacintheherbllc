How we work

A Florida supplier, selling food

Hyacinthe Herb LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L21000458328, filed in 2021, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

What we sell is food: herbs, spices and infusions. They are not medicines, they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and nothing we say has been evaluated by the FDA. Some botanicals do interact with prescription medication or are unsuitable in pregnancy, which is a reason to ask a pharmacist rather than a reason to believe a marketing claim. Everything is packed in a facility handling other botanicals, so read the allergen line.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will this help with sleep or digestion?

We will not tell you that. These are foods, not remedies, and claims of that kind are both regulated and usually unsupported. We will tell you how something tastes.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Ask your physician or pharmacist, genuinely. Several common botanicals are advised against in pregnancy, and that is not a question a herb supplier should be answering.

Can I take these with my medication?

Check with a pharmacist. Some botanicals interact with prescription medicines in ways that matter, and your pharmacist can check your specific list.

How fresh is it?

Every pack states origin and harvest. Dried herbs fade within about a year, which is why we sell sizes you will finish rather than catering tubs.

Do you do custom blends?

Yes, for cafés and small producers. We develop to a brief, sample until it is right, then supply consistently with the allergen data your labels need.

Are there allergens?

Stated on every label. We pack in a facility that handles other botanicals, so cross-contact is possible and we say so plainly.

Want to taste before buying?

Samples are free. Judge it by smell and taste rather than by a description.
